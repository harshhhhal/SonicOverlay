SonicOverlay

Manage visualizer settings and presets. Changes sync to open Spotify tabs instantly. To see live changes, keep Spotify open with capture running.

Why these permissions

  • tabCapture – analyzes the Spotify tab’s audio, only after you press Start.
  • activeTab – lets Chrome grant capture for the tab you invoked the extension on.
  • offscreen – hosts the Web Audio analyzer, which service workers cannot do.
  • storage – saves your settings and presets on this device.
  • open.spotify.com – draws the overlay and reads the visible track title, artist and cover.
  • i.scdn.co – fallback download of the cover image (image only) when the page blocks reading its colors.

Keyboard shortcuts

Defaults: Alt+Shift+V overlay, Alt+Shift+S panel, Alt+Shift+E edit mode, Alt+Shift+C capture. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
